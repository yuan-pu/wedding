Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent photographic paper cutout for the hero of a minimal wedding invitation website. Image 1 is the ONLY edit target: the uploaded square photograph of a smiling woman with round glasses on the LEFT and a smiling man in a pale yellow T-shirt on the RIGHT. Remove ONLY the original sky, buildings, trees and all other background, keeping the two photographed people together as ONE joined cutout. Preserve the exact original identities, facial geometry, expressions, eyes, teeth, glasses reflections, natural skin texture, hair, clothing, relative scale, positions and warm original sunlight. This is photo extraction, not a new portrait: do not redraw, beautify, restyle or invent faces, hands, clothing or missing body parts. Retain the original torso crop at the bottom and original side crops; do not reconstruct beyond the source. Add a clearly THICK warm ivory-white paper margin following the COMBINED OUTER SILHOUETTE of both people, roughly 35–50 pixels wide at a 1024px output scale. The outer edge should look hand torn from substantial matte paper: broad gentle uneven tears with small visible paper fibers, irregular but tasteful, not an evenly smooth sticker outline, not noisy serrations. The people and this thick paper backing are opaque; everything OUTSIDE the torn-paper silhouette is genuinely transparent alpha. No rectangular or square white backing, no background color, no checkerboard baked into the image, no scene, no typography, no decorations, no extra people. Leave generous transparent padding around the complete cutout and its paper edge so no border is cropped. Square canvas; faithfully preserve the original source photograph inside the cutout.
